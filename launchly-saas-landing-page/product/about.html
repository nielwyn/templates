About us

We're building calmer software

Launchly started as a side project between three friends tired of tools that got in the way of work.

Our story

We spent years at fast-growing startups, watching teams drown in tools that promised simplicity and delivered complexity instead. Launchly is our answer: software that gets out of the way so teams can focus on the work that actually matters.

Since launching, we've grown to serve thousands of teams across 40+ countries — without losing the focus on speed and simplicity that got us started.

Our values

What we stand for

Move fast

Speed is a feature. We ship often and sweat the details that make software feel fast.

Stay honest

Clear pricing, clear roadmaps, clear answers — even when the answer is "not yet."

Build together

Our best features started as customer feedback. We build in the open, with you.

Protect trust

Your data is yours. We treat security as a baseline, not a premium add-on.
